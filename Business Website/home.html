WELCOME TO CAFE NOIR

More Than Just
A Cup of Coffee.

Cafe Noir is a coffee shop located at Hollywood Terraces Building, KM23, Antipolo City. We serve different kinds of coffee, non-coffee drinks, frappuccino, and milk tea.

What makes Cafe Noir special is that aside from serving coffee, we also offer affordable rice meals that are suitable for students looking for a convenient place to eat.

Whether you need a caffeine boost or a refreshing drink, Cafe Noir is a perfect place to enjoy your favorite beverages.
